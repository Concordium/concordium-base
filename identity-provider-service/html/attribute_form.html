Identity, attributes, and credentials

Attributes describe a person or company. After verification and approval, an identity provider issues a Concordium ID. This test service skips those real-world checks and uses the attributes you supply. Your wallet can then use the issued identity to create account credentials for interacting with the chain on test networks.

What happens to my entries here?

Your supplied attributes are submitted to the test verifier and used to issue a Concordium ID containing that information. The resulting test identity is returned to your wallet for use on Concordium’s test networks. Use fictional information, not real personal or company details.
